About Diploma Campus

Empowering thousands of polytechnic students across all 24 districts of Jharkhand.

Diploma Campus was founded with a single mission: to eliminate the educational barrier for technical students in Jharkhand. Whether you are studying at Government Polytechnic Ranchi, GP Dhanbad, GP Adityapur, or a remote district institute, high-quality handwritten notes, past question papers, and entrance guidance should always be 100% free and easily accessible.

What We Offer:

  • Complete Semester Coverage: Notes, syllabus PDFs, and lab manuals for Semesters 1 through 6 across all major engineering branches.
  • Solved Question Papers: Authentic previous 6+ years' JUT Ranchi examination question papers with model answers.
  • D2D Lateral Entry Focus: Specialized preparation material for admission into B.Tech programs at premier institutes like BIT Sindri and UCET Hazaribag.
  • Interactive CBT Test Engine: Real-time computer-based testing with timers, scorecards, and ranking.
  • Vibrant Community Forum: Direct academic peer discussion and doubt clearance.
