About Diploma Campus

Empowering diploma students with free, high‑quality resources and a thriving learning community.

Our journey since 2023

We believe every diploma student deserves access to the best notes.

Diploma Campus started as a small Telegram group sharing handwritten notes. Today, it's a curated library of thousands of resources — from lecture PDFs to video explanations — all verified by seniors and faculty.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between classroom learning and exam preparation. Whether you're in Computer Science, Mechanical, Civil, or any diploma stream, we've got you covered.
