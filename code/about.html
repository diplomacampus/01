Our Story

Built by Students,
for Students

DiplomaCampus was born in a hostel room in 2022 when four engineering friends couldn't find quality notes online. Today we serve 50,000+ students across India.

The Problem We Solved

No More Searching Telegram Groups at Midnight

Before DiplomaCampus, engineering students relied on inconsistent Telegram groups, incomplete Google Drive folders, and handwritten notes photographed in poor lighting. Exam prep was a scavenger hunt.

We set out to build the definitive platform — every note, every branch, every semester, organized and searchable. And we've never looked back.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

2022

The Hostel Room Beginning

Four CSE students at NIT Trichy build the first version of DiplomaCampus over a weekend, hosting notes for their own branch as a shared Google Drive alternative.

2023

Going Multi-Branch

Added ECE, Mechanical, and Civil branches. Hit 5,000 registered users. Launched the first mobile-friendly redesign and introduced search functionality.

2024

Subscription Launch & Growth

Introduced Standard and Premium plans. Crossed 25,000 active students. Added previous year papers and launched the community Q&A feature.

2025

50K+ & Expanding

Surpassed 50,000 students, added video summaries, and launched AI-powered search. Expanding to regional language notes and adaptive study planners.
