Are the notes updated every semester?

Yes! Our team reviews and updates note packs at the start of every semester to match the latest syllabus. Pro subscribers always get the newest versions automatically.

Can I download the notes on my phone?

Absolutely. All notes are standard PDF files that work on any device — Android, iOS, Windows, Mac. You can annotate, highlight, and study offline.

What's the refund policy?

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee for subscription plans. For individual note purchases, refunds are reviewed case-by-case. Contact support within 48 hours of purchase.

How do I sell my own notes?

Sign up for the Creator plan, upload your notes, and our academic team will review them within 48 hours. Once approved, they're listed on the marketplace and you earn 80% of every sale.
