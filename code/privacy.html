Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 1, 2025 | Last Reviewed: April 10, 2026

1. Overview & Scope

Diploma Campus (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website diplomacampus.com (the “Site”) and interact with our educational resources, community channels (WhatsApp/Telegram), and services. By accessing or using the Site, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

This policy applies to all users, including prospective students, enrolled diploma candidates, and visitors. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please discontinue use of our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Personal Information Provided Voluntarily: When you contact us via email, subscribe to newsletters, or join our community groups, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, or student details. We only collect what is necessary.
  • Automatically Collected Data (Usage & Device): As you navigate the Site, we may collect certain information automatically, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, device identifiers, and clickstream data. This is used for analytics, security, and to improve user experience.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use essential cookies and analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics) to understand site traffic and enhance functionality. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.
  • Community Platform Data: When you join our official WhatsApp or Telegram channels, your profile name and phone number (as per platform settings) become visible to other members. We do not control the data processing within third‑party platforms.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for legitimate educational and operational purposes, including:

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving our Site and academic resources.
  • Responding to inquiries, support requests, and feedback.
  • Sending important updates, security alerts, and administrative messages.
  • Analyzing usage trends to optimize content delivery (e.g., most downloaded notes).
  • Protecting against fraudulent, unauthorized, or illegal activity.
  • Facilitating community interaction through our official groups (subject to platform terms).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files placed on your device. You may refuse to accept browser cookies by activating the appropriate setting; however, some parts of the Site may become inaccessible or not function properly.

We may use third‑party analytics providers (such as Google Analytics) that set cookies to help us analyze how users engage with the Site. These providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they use such information.

5. Third-Party Services & External Links

The Site may contain links to third‑party websites, plug‑ins, and applications (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Drive). Clicking on those links or enabling those connections may allow third parties to collect or share data about you. We do not control these third‑party websites and are not responsible for their privacy statements. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every site you visit.

6. Community Interaction (WhatsApp / Telegram)

Our student community operates via third‑party messaging platforms. When you join:

  • Your display name, profile picture, and phone number (if visible) may be seen by other group/channel members.
  • We strongly advise against sharing sensitive personal information (address, financial details) in these groups.
  • Diploma Campus is not liable for interactions or private messages exchanged between members outside of official announcements.
  • We reserve the right to remove any member who violates community guidelines or posts inappropriate content.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, please note that no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, but we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for users who are at least 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information. If you believe we might have any information from or about a child under 13, please contact us.

9. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to access, correct, or delete your data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond to your request in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

You can also opt out of receiving promotional communications by following the unsubscribe instructions in any email we send. Please note that you may still receive transactional or administrative messages.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The updated version will be indicated by an updated “Effective Date” and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
