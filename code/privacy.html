Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2023

1. Introduction

Welcome to Diploma Campus. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our students and users. This policy outlines how we handle information when you visit our website or join our community groups (WhatsApp/Telegram).

2. Information Collection

We do not collect personal identification unless you voluntarily provide it. We may collect non-personal data such as:

  • Device Information (Mobile/Desktop)
  • Browser type and IP address for analytics
  • Click patterns on notes and download links

3. Community Interaction

When you join our WhatsApp or Telegram channels, your phone number or username becomes visible to other members. Diploma Campus is not responsible for any private messages or interactions between students outside our official announcements.

4. Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your browsing session. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties.
