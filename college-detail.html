Admission Process & Counselling

  • Regular Entry (1st Year): Admission is based on Combined Merit List (CML) rank in the Jharkhand Polytechnic Entrance Competitive Examination (PECE) conducted by JCECEB.
  • Lateral Entry (2nd Year / 3rd Sem): 10% supernumerary seats for 12th Science / Vocational / 2-Year ITI passouts through JCECEB Lateral Entry exam.
  • JCECEB Counselling: Conducted online in multiple rounds (Round 1, Round 2, and Open / Mop-up round) with choice filling and seat allotment letter generation.
  • Documents Required: 10th Admit Card/Marksheet, PECE Admit Card & Rank Card, Residential & Caste Certificate (Jharkhand CO/SDO issued), and Income Certificate for TFW / e-Kalyan.
