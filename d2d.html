D2D Lateral Entry Exam Syllabus Breakdown

1. Engineering Maths (30 Qs)

Matrices, Determinants, Differential Calculus, Integral Calculus, Ordinary Differential Equations, Vector Algebra, Probability.

2. Engineering Mechanics (25 Qs)

Force systems, Equilibrium, Friction, Centroid, Moment of Inertia, Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work, Energy & Impulse.

3. Applied Physics (25 Qs)

Units & Dimensions, Elasticity, Surface Tension, Heat & Thermodynamics, Optics, Electrostatics, Magnetism, Modern Physics.

4. Chemistry & Comp (20 Qs)

Atomic structure, Electrochemistry, Metals & Alloys, Polymers, Environmental Chem, Fundamentals of Computers & C programming.
