Single state-level entrance test conducted by JCECEB Ranchi for matric / 10th students entering Government & Private Polytechnics across Jharkhand.

Eligibility: 10th Matric Pass (Min 35%)

Marking Rule: +1 Correct • -0.25 Negative
Top Targets: GP Ranchi, GP Dhanbad, GP Adityapur

High-Scoring Strategies from State Toppers

1. Master 5-Year PYQs

Over 60-70% of JUT semester theoretical & numerical questions are directly repeated or adapted from the last 5 years' question papers. Practice writing step-by-step solutions with neat circuit diagrams.

2. Focus on Formulas & Units

In PECE and D2D exams, fast mental math and formula recall in Engineering Mechanics, Calculus, and Applied Physics give you an immediate edge over competitors.

3. Timed Mock Test Drills

Attempt full-length CBT tests in our interactive test portal to eliminate negative marking and build speed management under real 150-minute exam constraints.
